Information security policy

This policy protects Acme's data and systems. It applies to every employee, contractor and intern.

Passwords

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and unique for every account. Use the company password manager to generate and store them. Never share a password, not even with IT.

Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on every company account. Use an authenticator app or a hardware security key; SMS codes are not allowed. Administrators must use a hardware security key.

Laptops and phones

  • Full-disk encryption must be switched on.
  • Screens lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
  • Install operating system security updates within 14 days of release.
  • Report a lost or stolen device immediately so it can be wiped remotely.

Data classification

Every document has one of four classifications. Handle it accordingly.

ClassExamplesWho may see it
PublicMarketing website, press releasesAnyone
InternalHandbook, team wikisAll employees
ConfidentialBudgets, customer contractsTeams that need it
RestrictedSalary data, health informationNamed people only

Reporting security incidents

Report a suspected security incident within one hour of noticing it, by email to the security team or in the security incidents chat channel. Examples: a phishing link you clicked, malware warnings, a lost laptop, or data sent to the wrong person. Do not try to investigate on your own.

Phishing

Forward suspicious emails with the "Report phishing" button in your mail client, then delete them. IT will never ask for your password by email or phone.

Working in public places

Use a privacy screen on trains and planes, do not leave devices unattended, and never charge company phones from public USB ports. Use the company VPN on public Wi-Fi.

Owner: Security team. Review cycle: yearly.
