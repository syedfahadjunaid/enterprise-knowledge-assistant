# Procurement process

How the finance team buys goods and services from suppliers.

## When you need a purchase order

Every purchase above 1,000 EUR needs a purchase order (PO) before you commit to the supplier. Raise the PO in the finance system; invoices without a PO number are sent back to the supplier.

## Approving a purchase order

Purchase orders up to 10,000 EUR are approved by the budget owner. From 10,000 to 50,000 EUR the department head also approves. Above 50,000 EUR the CFO approves and finance runs a competitive tender with at least three quotes.

## Onboarding a new supplier

A new supplier needs a completed supplier form, tax registration details and bank details. Finance verifies bank details by calling the supplier on a phone number from an independent source, never from the email that sent the details. This stops invoice fraud.

Suppliers that will handle Confidential or Restricted data must pass a security review before the contract is signed.

## Payment terms

Our standard payment term is 30 days from the invoice date. Finance runs payments twice a week, on Tuesdays and Thursdays.

## Software and subscriptions

Software purchases go through IT, even below the PO threshold, so licences are tracked and single sign-on is set up. Do not pay for software subscriptions with a personal card.
