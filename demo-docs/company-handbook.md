# Acme company handbook

This handbook applies to every Acme employee. If a team policy says something different, the team policy wins for that team.

## Working hours

Acme works with flexible hours. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time: be available for meetings during that window. Outside core hours, plan your day with your team. A full-time week is 38 hours.

## Remote work

You may work from home up to three days per week. Agree on your office days with your team so that people are in the office together.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 20 working days per calendar year, with your manager's approval in advance. Longer stays need approval from HR because of tax and social security rules.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 26 days of annual leave per year, on top of public holidays. Part-time employees get a proportional amount.

You can carry over at most 5 unused days into the next year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire.

Request leave in the HR portal at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick leave

If you are ill, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day. From the fourth consecutive day of illness you need a doctor's note. Sick days never count against your annual leave.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers get 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers get 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave can be taken in up to two blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Learning and development

Every employee has a learning budget of 1,500 EUR per year for courses, conferences and books, plus three learning days. Unused budget does not carry over. Your manager approves spending above 500 EUR in one go.

## Equipment

You get a laptop on your first day; laptops are replaced every three years. For working from home there is a one-time home office allowance of 500 EUR for a chair, desk or monitor.

## Code of conduct

Treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment and discrimination are never tolerated. Report concerns to your manager, to HR, or anonymously through the ethics hotline. Reports are investigated confidentially and retaliation is forbidden.
