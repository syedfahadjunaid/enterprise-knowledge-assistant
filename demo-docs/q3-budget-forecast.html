Q3 2026 budget forecast

Classification: Confidential. Prepared by Finance for the leadership team.

Summary

We forecast Q3 revenue of 18.4 million EUR, up 6 percent on Q2. Operating expenses are forecast at 14.1 million EUR, which gives an operating margin of 23 percent.

Hiring plan

The plan includes 12 new hires in Q3: 7 in engineering, 3 in sales and 2 in finance. Any hire beyond the plan needs CFO approval.

Cloud costs

Cloud infrastructure costs 1.1 million EUR per quarter. The target for Q3 is an 8 percent reduction compared with Q2, mainly from reserved capacity and deleting unused storage.

Risks

About 30 percent of revenue is billed in US dollars, so a weaker dollar lowers revenue in euros. Two large renewals are due in September; losing either would reduce Q3 revenue by about 0.6 million EUR.
