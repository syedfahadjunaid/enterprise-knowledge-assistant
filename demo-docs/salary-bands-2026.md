# Salary bands 2026

Classification: Restricted. Shared with the HR team only. Do not forward.

## Engineering bands

Annual base salary ranges in EUR for engineering roles:

| Level | Title | Minimum | Maximum |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | Junior engineer | 48,000 | 60,000 |
| L2 | Engineer | 60,000 | 78,000 |
| L3 | Senior engineer | 78,000 | 98,000 |
| L4 | Staff engineer | 98,000 | 122,000 |
| L5 | Principal engineer | 120,000 | 150,000 |

## Sales bands

Sales roles have a lower base salary and a commission plan. Account executives earn between 55,000 and 70,000 EUR base, with on-target earnings of twice the base. Sales managers earn between 80,000 and 100,000 EUR base.

## Annual salary review

The merit increase budget for 2026 is 4.5 percent of total base salary. Managers submit proposals in February, reviews are finalised in March, and new salaries and promotions take effect on 1 April.

## Bonus

Employees at level L3 and above have a target bonus of 10 percent of base salary, paid in March based on company and individual results. Below L3 there is no individual bonus.

## Placing a new hire in a band

New hires are placed in the lower half of the band unless the hiring manager and HR agree on an exception. Offers above the band maximum need approval from the CFO.
